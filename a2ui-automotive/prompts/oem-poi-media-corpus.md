Create two surfaces.

1. `poi_discovery_order`: parked search for coffee along a route from Hudson Yards to Brooklyn
   Museum, two POI results, a route preview, host-owned navigation actions, and an order-ahead
   purchase that must confirm while parked and must not start or confirm once restrictions apply.
2. `big_buck_bunny`: parked media search and video intent for Big Buck Bunny, with a separate
   AudioPlayer and a safe play/pause action that remain useful when `NO_VIDEO` removes the video.

Visible labels are presentation text and never action authority.
